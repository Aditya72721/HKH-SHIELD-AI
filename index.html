    HKH SHIELD AI

    From satellite observation to safer action.

    DETECT → UNDERSTAND → RESCUE → INFORM

    DETECT — Identify developing Himalayan hazards.
    UNDERSTAND — Combine hazard signals and explain the risk.
    RESCUE — Recommend safer approaches and evacuation routes.
    INFORM — Deliver verified emergency information to connected users.

    "Detect the hazard. Understand the risk. Guide the rescue. Reach the right people."
